IEC EN 62676-4 Camera Mapping & DORI Resolution Standards

Optical dimensioning benchmarks to guarantee computer vision task success at target inspection elevations:

DORI Level Minimum Pixel Density (PPM) Target Operational Goal Applicable Facility Zones
Identify ≥ 250 px/m Facial verification, vehicle license plate extraction (LPR), OCR Narrow FOV (≤ 60°), Ingress Gates, Weighbridges
Recognize ≥ 125 px/m PPE compliance (helmets, high-vis vests), personnel identification Standard FOV (80° - 100°), Storage Aisles, Robot Cells
Observe ≥ 62.5 px/m Forklift, pallet, and AGV transit tracking; crowd motion flow Wide FOV (90° - 110°), Cross-Aisles, Staging Bays
Detect ≥ 25 px/m Presence of human or vehicle in restricted zone, perimeter alarms Ultra-Wide FOV (106° - 180°), Outer Fence, Roofs

Optical Mathematics & 3D Ray Occlusion Modeling

1. Ground Pixel Density Formulation:
   PPM(D) = Resolution_Width / [ 2 · D · tan(HFOV / 2) ]
   Where D is line-of-sight slant range to target plane (1.5m AFF).

2. Under-Camera Blind Zone Trigonometry:
   D_blind = (Mount_Elevation - Target_Elevation) / tan(Tilt_Angle + VFOV / 2)
   Ensures zero-deadzone handoff between overlapping camera cones.

3. 3D Ray-Box Slab Intersect Occlusion Analysis:
   Physical obstacles (pallet racks, robotic cages, columns) cast geometric shadow
   cones. Longitudinal aisle alignment eliminates rack shadow occlusions along travel lanes.

12 Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

  • Procure and install cameras per layout
  • Configure network, VLANs, and VMS integration
  • Establish baseline lighting and environmental conditions
  • Deploy edge compute infrastructure

Phase 2: Model Development (Weeks 5-10)

  • Collect and annotate training data from camera feeds
  • Train/finetune detection models per use case
  • Validate model performance against acceptance criteria
  • Implement tracking and logic modules

Phase 3: Integration & Testing (Weeks 11-16)

  • End-to-end pipeline integration
  • Alert routing and notification configuration
  • User acceptance testing with stakeholders
  • Performance optimization and tuning

Phase 4: Deployment & Operations (Weeks 17-20)

  • Production rollout with monitoring
  • Documentation and runbook creation
  • Staff training and handover
  • Ongoing model retraining schedule
